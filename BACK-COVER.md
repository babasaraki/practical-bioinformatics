# Back cover

The copy below is what `scripts/make_back_cover.py` typesets. Keep it here as text so a
designer can reset it without transcribing from an image, and so the wording can be
reviewed separately from the layout.

## Category line (top)

> BIOINFORMATICS · COMPUTATIONAL BIOLOGY

A category heading in small caps at the top of the back cover is a trade convention: it
tells a bookshop where to shelve the book and a reader in two words what shelf they are
standing in.

## Headline

> **Bioinformatics is learned the way laboratory work is learned: by doing it.**

## Description

> This book teaches bioinformatics by doing it — making mistakes, and developing
> judgement about what the results mean.
>
> Every figure in these pages was generated by code printed in the book, running on data
> you download from public archives. Every command's output is shown as it was actually
> produced, including the places where a sensible default gives the wrong answer and you
> have to work out why. Nothing is illustrated with results that were never computed.
>
> It starts at the first command you type into a terminal and ends with a complete,
> reproducible analysis, written up to the standard a journal would expect.

## What you will do

> - Work in Unix, Python and R, and judge which one a problem calls for
> - Search GenBank, UniProt, Ensembl and ClinVar, and read what they return critically
> - Align sequences, call variants, annotate a genome, and test it against a curated
>   reference
> - Measure a protein structure, and read an AlphaFold confidence score honestly
> - Build networks, test enrichment, and spot a leaking machine-learning pipeline before
>   it reaches a manuscript

## Credentials line

> 16 chapters · 137 exercises with worked solutions · 49 computed figures · 256-term
> glossary · every dataset and script in the companion repository

## Photograph

`images/author-photo.jpg` is the supplied studio portrait (frame 2V2A9964), rotated
upright from its EXIF orientation and cropped head-and-shoulders to the box's 1.05:1.24
aspect, with the head filling about 55% of the frame and the surplus taken mostly from
below so the portrait keeps its headroom. Stored at 2094 x 2473 px, which is 1994 dpi
at the printed size — six times what print needs, so there is room to re-crop.

It was chosen over the two alternatives for the cover specifically: the studio backdrop
is close to the cover's own light grey ground, so the portrait sits on the page instead
of punching a hole in it, and the expression is open rather than formal, which suits a
book about learning. The lobby photograph has a dark warm background that fights the
grey, and the second studio frame (2V2A9965) is a shade more severe.

A keyline in the cover's gold is drawn inside the portrait's edge, so it reads as placed
rather than dropped in.

- **Barcode.** Amazon KDP overprints a barcode in a 2.0 × 1.2 in box at the bottom right
  of the back cover. The template leaves that box white. Do not place art or text in it;
  a dark background behind a barcode is a scanning failure.
- **Bleed.** 0.125 in on all four outer edges carries background colour only. Nothing
  that must be read may sit within 0.25 in of a trim edge.

## Specification, at the current page count

| | |
|---|---|
| Trim | 6 × 9 in |
| Extent | 591 pages (interior, cover plate excluded) |
| Spine | 591 × 0.002252 = **1.331 in (33.8 mm)**, black ink on white paper. The wrap in this package was rendered for 592 pp; regenerate it with `--pages` once a publisher confirms the final extent. |
| Full wrap, with bleed | 13.583 × 9.25 in = **4075 × 2775 px at 300 dpi** |
| Back panel, with bleed | 6.125 × 9.25 in = 1838 × 2775 px |

The spine width is a function of the page count, so rebuild the wrap if the extent
changes. Nothing may be printed on a spine narrower than 0.25 in; 1.333 in is
comfortable for the title, the author and an imprint.
